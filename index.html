Physics for AIWorkshop 2027

Exploring the principles, dynamics, and emergent behavior of modern AI. Two days of shared questions and open discussion.

Hong Kong Science ParkMarch 6–7, 2027

About

Physics for AI Workshop 2027 brings together researchers and students working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, physics, and applied mathematics. We will explore how ideas from statistical physics, dynamical systems, optimization, and representation learning can help explain how modern AI systems learn and behave.

The workshop is built around shared scientific questions. Short research introductions, poster discussions, and open discussions will give participants time to exchange ideas, examine unresolved problems, and explore possible collaborations.

We aim to create a relaxed and welcoming environment where participants can think aloud, ask questions, and share work in progress. We value curiosity and thoughtful discussion, with the focus on ideas rather than affiliation or seniority.

Topics

Learning objectives and physical principles
Free energy, entropy regularization, variational principles, and energy-based formulations of learning.
Generative models as dynamical systems
Diffusion, flow models, stochastic processes, and the dynamics of generation.
Representations and emergent behavior
Latent-space structure, geometry, symmetry, and emergent phenomena in foundation models.
Optimization and training dynamics
Loss landscapes, implicit bias, phase transitions, and mechanisms of generalization.

These themes are starting points for discussion. We also welcome related questions that connect different perspectives or challenge existing assumptions.

Format

Spotlight Talks

Brief, strictly timed 5–8 minute introductions to participants’ research and the questions they would like to discuss.

Poster Discussions

Face-to-face exchanges around ongoing work, preliminary ideas, and open problems.

Open Discussions

Extended discussions around shared scientific questions, with room for different viewpoints and emerging directions.

Informal Exchanges

Time to meet other participants, continue conversations, and explore research collaborations.
